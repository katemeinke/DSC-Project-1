# DSC-Project-1

## Data Preprocessing and Visualization: Analyzing Diagnostic Breast Cancer Data
For this project, I will be reviewing, cleaning, and visualizing a dataset on Breast Cancer Diagnostics from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. This data focuses on information about tumors found in breast cancer patients. The Features are computed from a digitized image of a fine needle aspirate (FNA) of a breast mass. The features describe characteristics of the cell nuclei present in the image such as radius, texture, perimeter, area, smoothness, compactness, concavity, concave points, symmetry, and fractional dimension. This data was recorded from 569 patients and has 30 features. These features are paired with a diagnosis of either malignant (cancerous) or benign (non-cancerous). The most notable features are mean texture, worst area, and worst smoothness.

## Research Goals:

Is there a correlation between certain aspects of a tumor and its ultimate diagnosis?
Are cancerous (malignant) tumors significantly different from benign tumors in size?
How can this data be used to identify commonalities or unique features of a tumor?
